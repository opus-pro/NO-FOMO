Show HN: Minimal LLM Post-Training Experiments on an 8GB GPU (SFT, DPO, GRPO)

A developer has released nano-llm-posttraining, an open-source framework designed for running LLM post-training experiments on consumer-grade hardware with as little as 8GB of VRAM. The repository provides minimal, simplified implementations of key alignment and reinforcement learning techniques, including Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). By utilizing optimization strategies like Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) and 4-bit quantization, the library allows researchers and developers to run advanced fine-tuning and reasoning-oriented post-training processes locally on a tight budget. Explorative modeling: Train on the best of K guesses

An article introduces explorative modeling, a novel training methodology designed to improve predictive accuracy and model adaptability by training on the best of K guesses. Instead of traditional single-path optimization, this approach generates multiple hypotheses during training and selectively updates model weights based on the top-performing candidate. This methodology helps the model navigate non-convex loss landscapes and avoid local minima. The technique is particularly beneficial for reinforcement learning, generative modeling, and complex sequence prediction tasks where uncertainty is high. (source: https://alexiglad.github.io/blog/2026/explorative_modeling/)
