Rufus-Air documents an eight-stage open post-training recipe

Rufus-Air presents a reproducible post-training pipeline for GLM-4.5-Air-Base, spanning supervised fine-tuning, reasoning and coding reinforcement learning, instruction following, agent capabilities, and RLHF. The authors describe data, rewards, infrastructure, stage ordering, and intermediate results. Their key argument is that reward reliability should guide the progression from verifiable tasks to softer judging signals. They report improvements over the official post-trained release; these remain paper-reported results. The work appeared before today but offers substantive implementation detail for open-model training. (source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29421)

AV-GRPO separates reward learning for joint audio-video generation

AV-GRPO proposes modality-anchored diffusion reinforcement learning to improve joint audio-video generation. The method separates coupled learning signals, freezes one modality tower during selected optimization, and adapts objectives to each modality’s dynamics. A companion dataset, 5DAV, supports controlled training across five dimensions. The authors report improvements over LTX-2.3 on JavisBench and VABench for quality, alignment, and synchronization. This earlier research item matters for teams investigating how reinforcement learning can improve multimodal generation without obscuring reward attribution. (source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29816)

ExplorationBench tests whether agents discover unfamiliar rules

ExplorationBench evaluates exploration in executable environments whose rules conflict with familiar knowledge, reducing the usefulness of simple recall. Its AlienCode and AlienLogic sandboxes each contain 70 tasks, with flawed manuals, environmental feedback, and tool-call interfaces. Across ten evaluated AI systems, the authors find that strong systems can learn unfamiliar rules, but longer exploration can stall or reverse gains. The paper predates today’s report and provides a useful evaluation design for agents that must acquire knowledge through interaction. (source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.30199)
