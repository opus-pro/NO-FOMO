ActiveSaddler adapts training curricula for agent harnesses

ActiveSaddler makes the choice of training scenarios part of agent-harness optimization. It groups recurring failures into reusable patterns, estimates where further practice can help, and balances known weaknesses against new failure discovery. The authors report Pass@1 gains of 4.4 points on GAIA2 and 7.5 points on Terminal-Bench 2.0 over the same optimizer with a fixed scenario order. The result suggests curriculum selection can matter alongside changes to prompts, tools, and control logic.

4Director controls generated video through rigid 3D geometry

4Director reconstructs objects from an input image as canonical meshes, then uses prescribed rigid transformations to control their movement over time. A depth-video scaffold and Motion Adapter translate this geometry into generated video while preserving appearance across views. The authors introduce a 20,774-clip training dataset and a metric that jointly measures motion adherence and identity preservation. Their reported improvements address a practical production challenge: predictable camera and object control without repeatedly reinventing hidden geometry.

Post-training can trade solution coverage for consistency

Sharpening Tax examines whether post-training improves single-attempt success while reducing the range of problems solvable through repeated sampling. Across fourteen base and post-trained model pairs, four families, and three agent benchmarks, the authors find this trade-off in many settings. They propose a diagnostic metric and posterior-tempered group sampling to reduce the cost. The practical lesson is to evaluate both single-shot accuracy and test-time scaling when choosing or training models for repeated agent attempts.

Argo-Bench tests enterprise data agents by consequences

Argo-Bench evaluates data agents on 210 tasks in a simulated food-delivery business backed by 235 tables and 7.5 billion rows. Agents must reconstruct business facts and take actions, such as allocating incentives or banning fraudulent accounts; grading uses the consequences inside the simulator. AgSpec reuses coding context for faster speculative decoding

AgSpec improves retrieval-based speculative decoding for coding agents by assembling reusable text from sessions, workspaces, and global corpora. It indexes files in the format agents emit and adjusts draft length using verification feedback. On two repository-level coding benchmarks, the authors report throughput gains up to 4.37 times at batch size one and 4.76 times at batch size sixteen over autoregressive decoding. These are generation-throughput results in the evaluated settings, rather than guaranteed end-to-end task speedups.

Align Then Reason evaluates lip sync before dubbed audio exists

Align Then Reason judges whether a candidate dubbing line matches visible articulation using silent video and text. It first aligns lip representations with phonetic units, then supplies local and global alignment evidence to a language model. The authors report substantial AUC improvements across model sizes on a seven-language benchmark, plus gains on real dubbing reranking and script-to-clip assignment. The approach targets both content and timing, a useful distinction for automated multilingual video production.
