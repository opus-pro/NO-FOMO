Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri with open weights and million-token context

Aleph Alpha released Kolibri, an English and German mixture-of-experts model with roughly 78 billion total parameters and 3 billion active parameters. Its official announcement offers full weights under Apache 2.0 and context support up to one million tokens. The company targets regulated enterprise and government work, emphasizing deployment control, document grounding, and configurable reasoning effort. Its reported benchmark results are vendor evaluations; teams should test the model on their own language, latency, and accuracy requirements.

06Oct 1

François Chollet frames reasoning models as a shift toward induction

François Chollet argues that the key difference between earlier base language models and modern reasoning models is a shift from intuiting an answer to intuiting a program or instructions that produce the answer. He explicitly separates this account from symbolic tool use alone. The post offers a conceptual framework for interpreting reasoning progress, not a controlled experimental result. It is useful for discussions about whether systems are learning reusable solution procedures or primarily producing plausible responses.
