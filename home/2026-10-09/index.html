SemiAnalysis questions China’s frontier safety disclosures

SemiAnalysis argues that China's AI governance prioritizes rapid development while concentrating regulation on applications and outputs. Its review of 857 model releases from nine developers found 31 with published developer safety results and nine with results available at launch. The authors explicitly distinguish missing public evidence from an absence of testing. The analysis highlights a policy gap between detailed application rules and disclosure obligations tied to frontier model capability or training scale.

01Oct 7

Learn2Play tests whether agents actually learn unfamiliar rules

Learn2Play Bench introduces text games with novel or counterintuitive rules to separate learning through interaction from reliance on pretrained knowledge. The authors report that retaining complete action and feedback histories can outperform summarizing experience into rules, while top human players still achieve higher peak scores. They also find that changing the agent harness can improve performance and reduce estimated inference cost with the same backbone. The benchmark puts memory design and exploration behavior under direct evaluation.

02Oct 7

Memento 3 compiles revisable rulebooks into world models

Memento 3 lets a frozen language-model agent learn explicit world models through external memory. It records hypotheses in a natural-language rulebook, compiles them into executable code, and revises them using prediction errors and replay checks. The authors report clearing all levels of 25 public ARC-AGI-3 games while using 44 percent of the human action count. These are results on the evaluated public games, not proof of general intelligence or performance on unseen private tasks.

03Oct 7

REMORY supplements compressed context with learned memory tokens

REMORY adds a bounded sequence of learned soft memory tokens to an agent's textual history summary. The network is trained to help a frozen language model approximate continuation with the full history. The authors report better source attribution on SummHay and a near full-context joint score using 5.2 percent of input positions. Agent benchmarks also show fewer repeated tool outputs and errors, suggesting that retaining information lost by summaries can improve long-horizon execution.

MiMo-V2.6 scales reinforcement learning across tasks and graders

The MiMo-V2.6 report describes an omni-modal model family trained by scaling reinforcement learning along batch throughput, environment diversity and grader compute. Its asynchronous setup consumes billions of tokens per step and supports contexts up to one million tokens. 05Oct 7

WorldGuide couples video generation with closed-loop task planning

WorldGuide treats procedural video generation as a loop of planning an action, generating its clip and inspecting the resulting visual state before continuing. A planner and executor learn from shared step-level demonstrations, while hierarchical visual memory limits history costs. The paper introduces roughly 59,000 annotated videos across 245 tasks and reports gains over MiniMax-H3 on two benchmarks. The contribution is tighter coupling between intended actions and generated outcomes, rather than visual plausibility alone.

OneSearch-VL grounds multimodal research in an evidence graph

OneSearch-VL unifies image and video deep research around a Visually Grounded Evidence Graph linking visual anchors, entities, supported facts and answer-producing operations. The authors use that structure for data generation, supervision and evaluation, including reinforcement-learning rewards for evidence traceability. Their 8B model improves over tool-enabled Qwen3-VL-8B by 20.2 and 17.6 percentage points on two new benchmarks. The results highlight explicit evidence structure as a design choice for multimodal research agents.

TokenRouter improves serving for token-level model routing

TokenRouter addresses the serving overhead of switching among language models at token granularity. Developers describe routing per request, while separate model subservers execute asynchronously and use delayed batching. The paper reports decoding throughput improvements ranging from 2.01 to 64.15 times over evaluated existing systems across model pairs and workloads. Those gains depend on the tested configurations; the broader contribution is a runtime designed specifically for fine-grained routing rather than a single-model execution assumption.
