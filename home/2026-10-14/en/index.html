WAVE-0 guides robot actions with lightweight latent futures

WAVE-0 augments a pretrained vision-language-action model with a 257-million-parameter world model that predicts action consequences in latent space. Rather than generating full video, it uses predicted future representations to condition action generation and trains with robot actions plus a counterfactual ranking objective. The authors report real-world task success rising from 38.3 to 73.3 percent over their baseline, alongside simulation results. The findings support testing lightweight predictive structure for robotic control.

Agent-Reflex trains recovery from noisy tool interactions

Agent-Reflex targets failures caused by noisy tool feedback and user corrections during long-horizon agent work. Its pipeline discovers reflection skills from paired successes and failures, applies reflective supervised fine-tuning, and then combines reinforcement learning with on-policy distillation. Built on Qwen3-8B, the system reports improvements across tool-use and noise benchmarks. The contribution is a training recipe for recovery behavior, rather than an assumption that a capable base model will reflect effectively on its own.

The Last Secure Code Benchmark evaluates 450 tasks derived from 150 real vulnerabilities across seven languages. ProRubric targets reward hacking in checklist-based RL

ProRubric examines how additive rubric rewards can encourage models to collect easy points while missing essential requirements. In clinical-consultation experiments, the authors find that higher rubric coverage can coexist with worse appropriateness.
