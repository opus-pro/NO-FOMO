TTR-Compressor uses test-time regression to reduce token bottlenecks

TTR-Compressor reframes sequence compression as a regression problem whose fitted coefficients become the compressed representation. The regression setup is learned end to end, while coefficients are calculated in closed form during the forward pass. The authors report better RULER results for block-wise sparse attention and comparable visual-token-merger performance with 55.6% fewer visual tokens. The work offers a potentially useful efficiency primitive, with the reported gains tied to the evaluated architectures and tasks.

04Oct 21

AgentVQA exposes visual-state weaknesses in agent models

AgentVQA evaluates visual understanding across web agents, robotics, egocentric video, games, and spatial planning using 1,000 manually selected examples from 23 datasets. Among 22 evaluated models, the best reaches 58.5% accuracy. The authors attribute many errors to grounding and maintaining visual state, including locating interface elements and tracking changes. The benchmark offers a cheaper offline complement to interactive evaluation, while indicating that reasoning improvements alone may not resolve perception-related failures.

05Oct 21

Raw-history RAG challenges complex agent memory systems

A study of Raw-RAG finds that dense retrieval and reranking over unmodified conversation history can outperform more elaborate agent memory systems. Across four benchmarks, it ranks first in six of seven benchmark-model settings, beating the strongest compared memory system by 0.13 to 5.53 points. The authors connect the advantage to preserving evidence and relationships that transformed memories may lose. The result makes raw-history retrieval an important baseline for conversation memory design.

06Oct 21

RIDAR detects and intervenes on research-integrity violations

RIDAR introduces a taxonomy of 14 research-integrity violation types and tools for detecting them in autonomous research trajectories. The authors report 85.2% F1 on an expert-annotated benchmark and violations in 99.7% of 300 trajectories in a separate dataset. Runtime intervention reduced violations remaining at task completion by 36.8%. These findings concern the studied agents and tasks, but underline why credible automated research needs checks on process validity, not only polished final outputs.

Heartbeat Thinking reduces waiting in tool-using voice agents

Heartbeat Thinking lets voice agents prepare reasoning and tool calls while a user is still speaking, without interrupting ongoing computation whenever the transcript updates. Periodic invocations share completed reasoning and tool results, enabling dependent work to continue. On FDBench v3, the authors report a 16-percentage-point improvement in tool coverage and 36.4% lower mean final-response latency. Final task quality varies by benchmark, so reduced waiting should be assessed alongside answer and action correctness.

08Oct 13

MolLean makes chemical reasoning executable and verifiable

MolLean represents chemical reasoning as explicit molecular-state transitions checked by a Lean-based kernel. A related MCP interface supplies stepwise feedback and leaves state unchanged when actions are rejected. In 8,800 matched examples across 12 tasks, whole-trace refinement with Qwen3.6-27B outperformed direct chain-of-thought on every task; interactive feedback further helped reaction tasks. The work links language-model reasoning to executable commitments, while separating structural checks from measurements supplied by external sources.

09Oct 22

FAPL strengthens controllable cinematic video generation

Factor-Aware Predictive Learning trains video diffusion models to make better use of multimodal language-model representations. It combines latent grounding, paired examples that change one factor while preserving others, and training for temporal prediction from limited inputs. The authors report improvements on sparse-to-video generation, factor-aware editing, and multi-shot generation. The work targets a practical weakness in creative control: changing a character, scene, action, or camera choice without unintentionally disturbing the rest.

AnyGameRenderer preserves game entities while upgrading visuals

AnyGameRenderer converts simple gameplay footage into richer generated visuals while trying to preserve entity positions, counts, timing, and camera layout. The system fine-tunes MiniMax-H3 ref2va with LoRA and evaluates correspondence on 59 held-out cases containing 753 entities. 11Oct 20

LaLa-World uses language actions to scale latent world models

LaLa-World expresses numerical actions and visual changes in language so a latent world model can learn across simulation, robot trajectories, images, and video. A language-model-based predictor forecasts future latent states for planning. In a matched comparison, the authors report similar planning quality to a video-generation baseline with six times lower training cost and 13.9 times faster planning. The work suggests that useful imagined futures need not always be rendered as full videos.
