01Oct 28

AB-Attention accelerates video diffusion with sparse attention

AB-Attention proposes a training-free approximation to dense attention for video diffusion transformers. It combines local query clustering, probability-weighted block sampling, and a kernel that packs smaller key/value blocks efficiently. On Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo, the authors report skipping 89–92% of attention and reaching up to 2.23 times end-to-end speedup while improving fidelity against the strongest compared baseline. These results make it a concrete inference-efficiency candidate, with benefits tied to the evaluated models and settings.

02Oct 28

JEPA research gives conditions for aligned representations

Randall Balestriero and Yann LeCun present a theorem connecting separately trained JEPAs across modalities when positive pairs are good views of the same scene. Their experiments report much greater sample efficiency for alignment than next-token prediction, including a 42-fold reduction in needed training samples in the compared setting. The central contribution is the role of positive-view construction. The theorem’s assumptions matter; the result should not be generalized into an unconditional guarantee for every model or dataset.

03Oct 28

Online RL trains UI agents directly on live iOS apps

This research separates UI navigation into planning and grounding components, then trains agents on live iOS applications using dense step-level rewards and terminal feedback. The framework combines PPO with a value-estimation method called GiGPPO and infrastructure for hundreds of virtual devices. The authors report stronger performance than supervised and terminal-only baselines, including nearly sevenfold Android generalization gains for an 8B agent. The findings support studying recovery through interaction instead of relying only on static demonstrations.

04Oct 28

L3G-World uses latent Gaussian memory for interactive worlds

L3G-World introduces a latent 3D Gaussian memory to preserve spatial consistency during interactive world generation. Memory grows with explored scene extent rather than the length of the generated stream, and latent features render directly into the video generator’s space without repeated RGB encoding. The authors report real-time generation at nine frames per second and substantial memory-operation improvements over compared baselines. The work targets a practical bottleneck in long-horizon world models: retaining geometry without continually expanding temporal context.

05Oct 24

Matched MCP and CLI tools still change agent behavior

A controlled study compares MCP and CLI interfaces built from the same semantic action contract and executed through the same runtime. Across six models and three environments, the authors find substantial behavioral and cost differences despite matched capabilities. Their DART approach preserves validated semantic decisions while repairing interface-specific realization, improving scores in all six evaluated settings. The practical implication is that tool presentation and feedback are experimental variables, not neutral wrappers around an otherwise unchanged agent.
