Reviso proposes an agentic video-captioning pipeline that starts with a low-frame-rate preview, builds a hierarchical observation plan, and selectively revisits important segments with tools. Training combines verified trajectories with reinforcement learning rewards for key facts and plan alignment, plus penalties for unproductive calls. The core idea is to allocate perception according to a video's content, rather than treating every visual detail as equally useful.

Continual harness evolution can erase earlier coding gains

A study of continual coding-agent harness evolution finds that improving on a new dataset can sharply damage performance on earlier tasks. In one reported configuration, Meta-Harness reaches 94.0% on SWE-Bench, then falls to 32.0% after further evolution on FeatureBench. The study spans three datasets and six evolution directions. Its implication is that changing the surrounding harness can produce forgetting even without changing model weights, so self-improvement needs regression checks across previously acquired capabilities.
