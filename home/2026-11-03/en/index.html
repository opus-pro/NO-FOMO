Library of Layers dynamically orders transformer blocks

Library of Layers proposes an architecture that chooses the order of transformer blocks at inference time rather than following a fixed stack. The authors report competitive text-pretraining performance against dense, recurrent, and mixture-of-experts baselines. In synthetic tasks, different routes correspond to different solution strategies, while multilingual experiments show language-dependent ordering. The research offers a way to study adaptive computation and controllability, with the reported results tied to the tested settings rather than universal efficiency gains.

Reviso learns where to look before writing video captions

Reviso is an agentic video-captioning framework that starts with a low-frame-rate overview, builds a hierarchical observation plan, and revisits relevant segments with tools. Its training combines verified trajectories with reinforcement learning rewards for key facts and plan alignment, plus penalties for unproductive tool use. The authors report improvements over evaluated open-source baselines on four captioning benchmarks. The core idea is selective evidence gathering: better captions can depend on choosing what to inspect, not simply processing more frames.

Build Your Own DLM proposes a LoRA-only recipe for converting existing autoregressive language models into diffusion models while freezing base parameters.
