SemiAnalysis visits an 800MW AI campus in Inner Mongolia

SemiAnalysis describes a visit to a wind-powered, 800MW campus in Inner Mongolia, with 120MW reportedly built within a year. Its overview also highlights a 5MW pilot using solid-state transformers and 800V direct current, alongside cooling without central chillers. The account connects AI infrastructure expansion to power conversion and thermal design. llama-modchip claims 284B DeepSeek inference within 64GB RAM

The llama-modchip repository claims it can run a 284-billion-parameter DeepSeek model on a CPU system with 64GB of RAM. Its documentation attributes the memory reduction to compression, quantization, and specialized inference kernels, and publishes hardware-specific performance tables. These are project-reported results rather than independently reproduced measurements. The project is worth watching because fitting large mixture-of-experts models on existing workstations could expand local inference options, provided quality and speed hold up in external tests.
