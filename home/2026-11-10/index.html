FewStepRL develops a framework for adapting reinforcement-learning objectives to few-step diffusion models, whose interval-based predictions differ from the instantaneous quantities used by many existing objectives. The authors implement few-step versions of several established methods in one modular codebase. The work targets the cost of repeated policy rollouts while preserving the distinctions among objectives rather than treating all diffusion policies identically.
