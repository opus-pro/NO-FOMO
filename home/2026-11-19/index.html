AnyS1 routes routine agent decisions to a 4B model

AnyS1 turns procedural skills into reusable decision points that a lightweight model can resolve before escalating uncertain cases to a larger model. The researchers mine more than a thousand public skills and train a calibrated 4B pointer model on execution-grounded states. 03Nov 17

PIPETTE diversifies prompt-injection training attacks

PIPETTE studies why successful automated prompt-injection attacks do not always produce stronger defenders after adversarial training. The authors identify mode collapse in attack discovery and introduce a dictionary-learning reward to encourage broader strategy coverage, alongside on-policy defender training. On AgentDojo and ASB, they report strong resistance to static and adaptive attacks while preserving legitimate task utility. The work shifts attention from attack success alone to the diversity and training value of discovered attacks.

04Nov 17

StrideWorld exposes drift in interactive world models

StrideWorld evaluates interactive video worlds against references fixed before the motion being tested, reducing the chance that a benchmark absorbs scene deformation into its own reference. It separately examines scene rigidity and action consistency. Across 140 starting views, the authors report deformation in all but one evaluated model and frequent problems on composed routes. The benchmark makes persistent geometry and predictable movement explicit requirements, complementing the visual appeal emphasized in many world-model demonstrations.

05Nov 16

Study questions whether agent evolution can detect real gains

The Resolution Hypothesis argues that self-improving agents often stall because noisy evaluations cannot reliably distinguish useful changes from harmful ones. Across seven evolution methods, six models, and more than ten million model calls, the authors find selection errors at both candidate and method-ranking levels. Their results suggest that improved evaluation resolution may matter more than generating additional candidate prompts, skills, or memories. Reported rankings can otherwise reflect variation between runs rather than durable progress.

Code World Model 2.0 introduces Invoker-Render, combining a bidirectional creation model with a causal four-step streaming renderer. 07Nov 15

VAE Rhythm detects temporal traces of generated video

VAE Rhythm looks for periodic traces introduced when video generators decode groups of frames with a causal VAE. A phase scan measures reconstruction-error periodicity without requiring detector training. The authors test 39 variants of 31 generators and use the signal both directly and to improve existing detectors. They report gains across multiple benchmarks, suggesting that generation-pipeline traces can complement visible artifacts, though performance remains tied to the tested models and datasets.

08Nov 9

Tideway coordinates RAM and VRAM for local MoE inference

Tideway targets large mixture-of-experts models on consumer devices whose weights spill across GPU memory, host RAM, and SSD storage. It treats RAM and VRAM as a coordinated pool with layouts adapted to prefill and decoding, plus CUDA Graph execution under expert paging. The authors report average improvements of 1.97 times in end-to-end latency and 2.40 times in time to first token against the fastest baseline, making memory management central to local deployment.
